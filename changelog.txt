EverlastingWinter Changelog
===========================

1.2.0-NeoForge-mc26.1.2
-----------------------
New
 - Leaves in the snow biomes now get a pale, frosty green tint instead of a flat grey
 - New config option frostedLeavesColor to pick your own leaf tint as hex code (#RRGGBB)
 - New config option frostedLeaves to turn the leaf tint off and keep the vanilla leaf colors. Both leaf options take effect when the world or server starts
Fixes
 - Snow golems, endermen and other mobs that are hurt by water (also from other mods) no longer drown under the open sky while it snows
 - Snow golems no longer melt in snowy deserts, savannas and badlands
Changes
 - Snowfall no longer counts as rain, so the trident's Riptide enchantment only works in real rain, like in vanilla

1.1.2-NeoForge-mc26.1.2
-----------------------
Fixes
 - Endermen no longer act as if they were standing in rain in the End, the Nether and dry biomes like deserts while constantSnowfall is on, so they stop taking damage and teleporting around nonstop

1.1.1-NeoForge-mc26.1.2
-----------------------
Fixes
 - Snow no longer piles up on iron bars, glass panes, chains, lanterns and other blocks without a full top surface
Changes
 - With breakCropsAndFarmland turned off, snow no longer settles on empty farmland
